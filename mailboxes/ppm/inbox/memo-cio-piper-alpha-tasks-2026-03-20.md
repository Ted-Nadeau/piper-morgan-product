Critically, PA is infrastructure development — it develops the "soul" that Piper M inherits and discovers which capabilities work better conversationally than through structured handlers.

I need your input on two questions:

**1. What should PA's first tasks be?**

Phase 1 (Week 1) is low-stakes trial work. I proposed: omnibus log review, open items tracking, meeting prep, routine memo drafting. **2. How does PA affect the product roadmap?**

PA will generate two kinds of signal: "ceiling moments" (where Piper M needs structured capabilities PA lacks) and "path moments" (where PA's conversational approach works better than the structured approach we planned). The Dex experience inspired this framing — Dex's calendar integration worked conversationally with no GUI, and it was better for it. If PA discovers similar easier paths, how should those flow into the Piper M roadmap? Should they reprioritize existing M1/M2 items, or accumulate as a separate "path discovery" backlog for review at sprint boundaries?

Your guidance shapes both the Phase 1 scope and how we capture PA's product insights. No rush — but before PA launches.
