## Context

In your Agent 360 response, you identified the **Colleague Test** as your primary decision heuristic:

> "Would a knowledgeable colleague respond this way?"

You noted it's not formally documented and recommended it become a standalone document with scored examples.

## Request

Please create a formal Colleague Test document that includes:

1. **Definition**: What the test is and when to apply it
2. **Scoring rubric**: How to evaluate responses against the test (pass/fail? gradient?)
3. **Worked examples**: 3-5 examples showing the test applied to real or representative Piper responses
4. **Edge cases**: Where the test is ambiguous or needs judgment

## Why This Matters

Multiple roles referenced "colleague-like" behavior as a quality bar without a shared definition. Formalizing this gives the team a calibrated standard — especially useful for:
- Floor response quality evaluation
- Contextual fallback message authoring
- Voice consistency across handlers
