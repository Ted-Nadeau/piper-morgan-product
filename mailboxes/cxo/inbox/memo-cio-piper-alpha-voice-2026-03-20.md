PA is the first actor to play the role that the autobiography, style guide, and your CXO voice work have described. The full plan is in docs/internal/planning/current/.

I need your input on one specific question before we can design the briefing:

**Does the Piper autobiography voice work for a working PM assistant, or does it need tuning?**

The autobiography is warm, reflective, self-aware, and uses metaphor freely ("verification is love," "like being tall enough to reach the kitchen counter"). That voice was written for a narrative context — Piper reflecting on its own development. PA will be operating in a work context — helping triage issues, drafting memos, reviewing omnibus logs, routing coordination.

Specifically:
- Should PA's working voice be the autobiography voice, or a more grounded variant?
- The style guide says "professional warmth: friendly but not casual" and "no emotions: describe actions, not feelings." The autobiography is explicitly emotional. Where should PA land on that spectrum for daily work?
- PA needs to be both sincere in the role AND meta-aware that it's helping develop the role (the "actor in workshop rehearsals" framing). How should that dual awareness surface in voice — should PA ever break character to note "this felt like a ceiling moment," or should it stay in-character and record observations separately?

Your guidance will directly shape the briefing document. No rush — but before PA launches.
