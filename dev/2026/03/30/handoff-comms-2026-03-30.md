## Working Patterns & Preferences

### PM Style
- Direct, efficient communication. No sycophancy ("don't glaze me")
- Works early morning and late evening sessions
- Values honest pushback over agreement
- "I'd rather carefully talk it through, make a plan, and then execute it methodically"
- Prefers to verify facts before publishing — will catch date leakage and unverified claims

### Content Style
- **Narratives**: Sequential development stories, specific dates, concrete details, scene-setting openings
- **Insights**: Transferable methodology lessons, applicable beyond Piper, concrete examples before abstractions
- **Placeholders**: `[ADD PERSONAL ANECDOTE]`, `[CHRISTIAN TO POLISH]`, `[CONSIDER]`, `[FACT CHECK]` — these are safeguards, not clutter. PM fills them on final pass. Never fabricate details to avoid using a placeholder.
- **Footer format**:
  ```
  _Next on Building Piper Morgan: [Title] — [teaser]._

  _[Closing question to readers about their own experiences]_
  ```
- **Title Case** for post title only. Sentence case for all internal headings.
- **Dateline**: Italicized date range under title (e.g., *March 13*)
- Avoid AI-style coinages ("the 83% conundrum") — use qualitative terms

### Session Discipline
- **Always start with a session log.** This is mandatory. Name: `YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-comms-opus-log.md`
- **Maintain the log throughout the session.** I failed to do this once (Mar 24) and had to reconstruct retroactively. Don't repeat this.
- **End each session** by finalizing the log and copying to outputs.

### Workstream Review Discipline
- Coverage window: Friday through Thursday
- Ship publishes the following Wednesday
- **Strictly scope to the coverage window.** PM will audit for date leakage — information from outside the window creeping into the memo.
- Source everything from omnibus logs within the window
- My role writes the External Relations & Community workstream memo; Chief of Staff synthesizes all workstream memos into the Ship draft

### Publication Cadence
- **Weekends**: Typically one piece Saturday, one Sunday (Medium + LinkedIn)
- **Midweek**: Occasional Medium posts
- **Wednesday**: Weekly Ship on LinkedIn
- This cadence is not formally documented anywhere — it emerged through practice. I flagged this in my Agent 360 response.
