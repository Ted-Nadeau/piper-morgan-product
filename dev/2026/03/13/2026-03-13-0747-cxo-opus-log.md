### 2. Workflow Hijack UX Guidance Memo
Reviewed Lead Dev's guidance request (`2026-03-12-hijack-ux-guidance-request.md`) on #888/#889 and wrote CXO response memo for PPM review.

**Key recommendations**:
- **Escape**: Combination approach — off-topic detection primary, explicit commands backup, timeout safety net. Off-topic detection is the colleague-pattern answer; timeout-only or commands-only are tool patterns.
- **Re-entry**: Save state, offer to resume once, accept "no" gracefully. Don't nag. Save partial standups.
- **Activation**: Offer-first for onboarding (aligning with PDR-001 "first recognition" philosophy). Explicit invocation for standup. Auto-activate is a wizard wearing a conversational mask.
- **Standup scope**: Structural completion + "done" recognition + save partials on interruption.
- **Root principle**: The session belongs to the user, not the workflow. Workflows are guests in the user's session.

Identified this as Pattern-062 (Assembly Assumption) at UX layer — ProcessRegistry works as designed per ADR-049, but the composed experience traps users. ADR-049 risk table had flagged this; mitigation wasn't implemented.
