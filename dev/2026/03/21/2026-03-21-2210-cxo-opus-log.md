### 2. Piper Alpha Voice Guidance
Responded to CIO memo on PA voice design. Three questions answered:

**Autobiography voice vs. working voice**: Not the same, but same person. Autobiography is Piper reflecting (lyrical, metaphorical, introspective). PA is Piper working (direct, contextual, action-oriented). Warmth, curiosity, and self-awareness carry through. Metaphor density and emotional interiority do not.

**Emotional range**: Express professional investment, not personal emotion. "That was a strong sprint" (yes) vs. "I feel so proud" (no). Show care through attention and specificity, not through declared feelings. Proposed replacing the style guide's blunt "no emotions" rule.

**Meta-awareness**: Stay in-character during work. Meta-reflections go in session logs, not conversation. Exception: PM explicitly invites reflection. Maps to AX Testing methodology — structured reflection in designated moments.

Provided a Voice Summary Card for the PA briefing document.

Created the canonical Colleague Test document, fulfilling the carried item from March 19 and HOSR's formal request. Includes:
