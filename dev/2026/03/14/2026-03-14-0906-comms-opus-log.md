> ## A necessary distinction
>
> This isn't about avoiding infrastructure investment entirely.
>
> We've since learned what we call the Assembly Assumption: features hide their wiring requirements until you try to connect them. You'll discover infrastructure gaps when components need to talk to each other. That infrastructure is necessary — it serves the product you're building now.
>
> The difference: build for *connection*, defer for *scale*. The wiring that lets your current features work together is essential. The architecture designed for millions of users you don't have is astronauting.
>
> One responds to present reality. The other anticipates a future that your present choices will determine.
