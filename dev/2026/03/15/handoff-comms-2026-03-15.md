## Working Patterns & Preferences

### PM Style
- Direct, efficient communication
- No sycophancy ("don't glaze me")
- No "Hey team" greetings
- Works early morning and late evening sessions
- Values honest pushback over agreement

### Content Style
- **Narratives**: Sequential development stories, specific dates, concrete details
- **Insights**: Transferable methodology lessons, applicable beyond Piper
- **Footer format**:
  ```
  ---

  _Next on Building Piper Morgan: [Title] — [teaser]._

  _[Closing questions]?_
  ```
- Avoid AI-style coinages ("the 83% conundrum") — use qualitative terms
- Placeholders are safeguards, not clutter — PM fills them on final pass

### Session Discipline
- Start each session with a log
- End each session by completing the log and copying to outputs
- Log naming: `YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-comms-opus-log.md`
