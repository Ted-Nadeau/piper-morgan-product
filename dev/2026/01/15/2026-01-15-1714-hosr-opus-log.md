| Completion Criteria Enforcement | PATTERNS EXIST | 045, 046, 047 documented but enforcement inconsistent |

### HOSR Feedback on the Memo

**1. The memo is accurate but static.**
It captures the state as of Jan 5. The last 10 days demonstrated these patterns working in practice, but also revealed gaps:
- Mailbox system (Jan 13) is a new coordination mechanism not in the memo
- Pattern-049 (Audit Cascade) addresses verification gaps not mentioned
- The "PM intervention" pattern (Jan 9) isn't documented as a coordination mechanism

**2. Subagent Deployment Protocol is the highest-priority codification.**
Jan 9 showed 3x speedup. This is real value. But it happened inside Lead Dev's context—if not documented well, the pattern can't be replicated or improved. Protocol should cover:
- When to deploy (independent tasks, investigation, parallel fixes)
- How to deploy (task types, prompt structure)
- What to report (evidence requirements)
- Cost considerations (Haiku cheap, Opus coordination expensive)

**3. The 75% Pattern needs enforcement, not just documentation.**
It's mentioned in the memo as an anti-pattern. But Jan 9 and Jan 12 showed it still happens. The countermeasures (Patterns 045-047) exist but aren't consistently applied. HOSR could play a role in monitoring for this pattern across sessions.

**4. Handoff discipline remains the weakest link.**
The memo notes "Still relies on session logs rather than structured protocol." This is still true 10 days later. Cross-session handoffs depend on PM manually carrying context. This is the constraint that limits HOSR's ability to track agent health.

### Questions for Discussion

1. **Should HOSR own the Subagent Deployment Protocol?** The Lead Dev memo recommends HOSR codify it, but it's deeply technical. Is this within HOSR scope, or should Lead Dev own it with HOSR input?

2. **What's the enforcement mechanism for 75% Pattern?** Patterns 045-047 exist but aren't preventing the problem. Is this a human task, an agent task, or a process task?

3. **Mailbox system**: Is this intended for HOSR use? The Jan 13 implementation could enable async agent-to-agent coordination that HOSR could monitor.
