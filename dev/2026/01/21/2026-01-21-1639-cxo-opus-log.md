### 6:40 PM - Response Memo Drafted

Created `memo-lead-dev-consciousness-templates-response-2026-01-21.md` with guidance on all five issues:

**Overarching principle**: Piper is a colleague, not a character. Warmth should feel professional, not performative.

**MEDIUM Issues**:

| Issue | Key Guidance |
|-------|--------------|
| Onboarding | Professional-warm; one first-person intro ("Hi, I'm Piper Morgan"), then shift to "we"; avoid minimizers ("just"), overpromising, exclamation overuse |
| Confirmations | Tone proportional to reversibility — calm for low-stakes, serious for irreversible; match emotional weight to actual consequences |
| Session timeout | "Still there?" framing + protective language ("to keep your account secure"), not policy justification ("for your security") |

**HARD Issues**:

| Issue | Key Guidance |
|-------|--------------|
| Toast system | JS-side message map (not backend-driven) — simpler, no latency; neutral voice for toasts (not first-person); ~2-3h |
| Validation | Minimal personality — validation is friction, don't add commentary; avoid "oops" and blame language; "needs" instead of "required" |

**The Contractor Test applied**:
- A new contractor would introduce themselves warmly but professionally (onboarding ✓)
- A contractor wouldn't announce "I've filed your TPS report!" — they'd just confirm it's done (toasts ✓)
- A contractor wouldn't say "Oops, you forgot the cover sheet!" — they'd note what's needed (validation ✓)

**Prioritization if time-constrained**: Onboarding → Confirmations → Session timeout → Toast system → Validation
