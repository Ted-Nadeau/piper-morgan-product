## Key Consciousness Concepts Found

### Five Orientation Queries (The Core Framework)

From PM-070, the foundational consciousness model:

1. **Identity**: "Who am I?"
   - Purpose: Self-awareness and role definition
   - Embodied AI function: Establishing presence and identity
   - Questions: "What's your name?", "What can you do?", "What makes you different?"

2. **Temporal**: "When am I?"
   - Purpose: Time consciousness and historical context
   - Embodied AI function: Sequential awareness and temporal positioning
   - Questions: "What day is it?", "What did we accomplish yesterday?", "What's the agenda today?"

3. **Spatial**: "Where am I?"
   - Purpose: Context awareness and position in workspace
   - Embodied AI function: Environmental consciousness and project landscape awareness
   - Questions: "What projects are we working on?", "Which project should I focus on?", "Where are we in the lifecycle?"

4. **Capability**: "What can I do?"
   - Purpose: Agency and action capability
   - Embodied AI function: Intentional execution and practical engagement
   - Questions: "Create a GitHub issue", "Analyze this document", "Generate a status report"

5. **Predictive**: "What should happen?"
   - Purpose: Foresight and strategic guidance
   - Embodied AI function: Pattern recognition and wisdom
   - Questions: "What should I focus on today?", "What patterns do you see?", "What risks should I be aware of?"

### The Flattening Discovery

From Nov 25 analysis, the exact point where consciousness was lost:

| Original Vision (PM-070) | Implementation (Oct 2025) | Translation Loss |
|--------------------------|---------------------------|------------------|
| Identity = Self-awareness | Identity = Capability list | Lost presence and relationship |
| Temporal = Time consciousness | Temporal = Date string | Lost meaning from temporal data |
| Spatial = Context awareness | Spatial = PIPER.md lookup | Lost spatial understanding |
| Capability = Agency | Capability = Handler routing | Lost intentionality |
| Predictive = Foresight | Predictive = Hardcoded priorities | Lost prediction ability |

**Key Quote from Nov 25**: "The words survived but the meaning got flattened"

### Six Consciousness Patterns from Morning Standup

Evidence that consciousness CAN be preserved (from P0 Analysis):

1. **Present Moment Awareness** - Piper knows what's happening NOW
   - Implementation: "Currently in: Meeting (ends in 15 min)"

2. **Contextual Encouragement** - Response warmth calibrated to observed context
   - Implementation: 4-tier encouragement levels based on accomplishment

3. **Time Perception with Meaning** - Time gets emotional context
   - Implementation: "Lightning fast ⚡", "under target", "optimize me"

4. **Graceful Degradation with Awareness** - Failures acknowledged honestly
   - Implementation: "I can't reach GitHub. Suggestion: Check token in PIPER.user.md"

5. **Multi-Source Integration** - Synthesis from GitHub, Calendar, Documents
   - Implementation: Parallel gathering and unified narrative

6. **Supportive Reframing** - "Challenges to work through" not "Blockers"
   - Implementation: Language choice that preserves integrity and hope

### Object Model Grammar (ADR-045)

Formal foundation for consciousness preservation:

**"Entities experience Moments in Places"**

- **Entities**: Actors with identity and agency (people, AI, teams, projects)
- **Moments**: Bounded significant occurrences with theatrical unities
- **Places**: Contexts where action happens (channels, repos, offices)
- **Situations**: Container holding sequences of Moments

**Eight Perceptual Lenses**:
1. Temporal (when, sequence, deadline)
2. Hierarchy (containment, levels)
3. Priority (urgency, importance)
4. Collaborative (who's involved)
5. Causal (what causes what)
6. Contextual (which context applies)
7. Flow (movement, state changes)
8. Identity (what is this)
