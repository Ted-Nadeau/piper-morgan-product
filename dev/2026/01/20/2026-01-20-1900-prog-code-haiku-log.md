### From ADR-045 (Formal Object Model)

**Grammar Foundation**: "Entities experience Moments in Places"
- Entities: Actors with identity (people, AI, teams, projects)
- Moments: Bounded significant occurrences
- Places: Contexts where action happens
- Situations: Container for Moment sequences

**Ownership Model**:
- Native: Created/owned by Piper (Sessions, Memories, Concerns)
- Federated: Observed and acted upon (GitHub Issues, Slack, Calendar)
- Synthetic: Constructed through reasoning (Inferred Risks)

**Eight Perceptual Lenses**:
1. Temporal (when, sequence, deadline)
2. Hierarchy (containment, levels)
3. Priority (urgency, importance)
4. Collaborative (who's involved)
5. Causal (what causes what)
6. Contextual (which context applies)
7. Flow (movement, state changes)
8. Identity (what is this)
