## Patterns Observed

### Verbosity Backfire Pattern
More detailed instructions can be LESS effective than simple triggers. Causes: skimming, cognitive overload, deferral. Applies to any protocol that must be followed reliably.

**Implication**: When formalizing protocols, prefer simple memorable triggers with details in loadable skills.

### Compaction Boundary Pattern
Context compaction is a hard boundary where progressive loading fails. Protocols that must survive compaction need inline CLAUDE.md presence—but keep it SIMPLE.

### Architecture Validation
The "simple trigger + detailed skill" architecture is now validated by:
1. Theory (skill vs protocol distinction from Jan 24)
2. Counter-evidence (detailed protocol failures Jan 22-25)
3. Proposed fix (Docs Agent's analysis)
