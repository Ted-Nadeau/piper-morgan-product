### The MUX Object Lifecycle Model

From ADR-055 and the original sketches, the **8-stage lifecycle** is:

```
EMERGENT → DERIVED → NOTICED → PROPOSED → RATIFIED → DEPRECATED → ARCHIVED → COMPOSTED
    ↑                                                                              |
    └─────────────────────────── feeds new ────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Stage | Meaning | Experience Phrase |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| EMERGENT | First stirrings of formation | "I sense something forming..." |
| DERIVED | Pattern recognized from emergence | "I recognize a pattern emerging..." |
| NOTICED | Brought to attention | "This has caught my attention..." |
| PROPOSED | Formally recommended | "I am considering this proposal..." |
| RATIFIED | Officially accepted and active | "This is now part of our established reality" |
| DEPRECATED | Marked for retirement | "This served us well, but its time is passing" |
| ARCHIVED | Preserved but inactive | "This rests in memory, preserved..." |
| COMPOSTED | Transformed into nourishment | "This has transformed into nourishment for future growth" |

**Key insight**: Composting is **not deletion—it's transformation**. Composted objects feed new EMERGENT ideas.

### Hard Objects vs. Soft Objects (from Jan 27 discovery)

| Hard Objects (with lifecycle) | Soft Objects (no lifecycle) |
|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Todo, Project, WorkItem, Feature, Document, Conversation | Insight, File |

**Insight Lifecycle Decision (Jan 27)**: Insights are "composted output," not entities. They don't get lifecycle states. The grammar test: "What would 'BLOCKED insight' mean?" → Category error.

## Conversational Glue Research & Implementation Guide

### Research Completed (4:49 PM)

Conducted comprehensive research on conversational AI design patterns. Key sources:
- Stanford Speech & Language Processing (dialogue management)
- Google Conversation Design guidelines
- Amazon Alexa design best practices
- Microsoft Copilot Studio CUX principles
- Rasa CALM framework
- Academic research on multi-turn dialogue, topic shifts, repair strategies
- Production system analysis (Notion AI, Linear, Copilot, Slack AI)

### Key Research Findings

**Paradigm Shift**: Modern conversational AI should use **LLM-native patterns** that abandon rigid intent classification in favor of flexible dialogue management with confidence-tiered responses.

**The Five Foundational Principles**:
1. Conversation as Collaboration, Not Interface
2. Implicit Over Explicit (confirmation)
3. Information Flows Forward (never re-ask)
4. Graceful Over Rigid (handle unexpected)
5. Trust Governs Autonomy

**Critical Patterns Identified**:
- Confidence-tiered response selection (4 tiers)
- Multi-intent handling with execution planning
- Follow-up recognition with lens inheritance
- Natural slot filling (not interrogation)
- Progressive disclosure in workflows
- Repair strategies that provide options

**Anti-Patterns to Eliminate**:
- Parrot confirmations
- "Is that your main project?" templated questions
- Button language
- Generic "I don't understand" errors
- Dead ends without alternatives
