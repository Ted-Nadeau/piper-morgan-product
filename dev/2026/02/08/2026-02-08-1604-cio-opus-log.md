## Transcript Analysis: Dan Heck Call (Feb 5)

### TUG's Core Ethics Framework

**The HAFF Framework** (Human Aligner Forever):

1. **Cultivate Human Agency**: Help people have good choices they understand
2. **Communicate Holistically**: Whole truth, factual + ethical + authentic
3. **Reflect on Whole Systems**: Gather information, get system view, create virtuous cycle

**Three Validity Claims** (from Habermas):
| Claim | Description | AI Challenge |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Factual** | Is it true? | AI can do this |
| **Ethical** | Is it aligned with group ethos? | AI can do this |
| **Authentic** | Does it come from genuine experience? | AI cannot fully do this |

**Key Insight**: Sam Altman's AGI definition ("do any work a person could do") is problematic because authenticity-requiring work cannot be done by AI. This creates a predictive ethical framework.

### Vocabulary Mapping: TUG ↔ Piper Morgan

| TUG Concept | Piper Equivalent | Notes |
|-------------|------------------|-------|
| **Warranted Trust** | **Trust Gradient / ADR-053** | Exact alignment — trust should match reality |
| **Ethical Pluralism** | **Multi-Agent Consensus** | Multiple ethical frameworks allowed |
| **Baselines (Red Lines)** | **Inviolate Boundaries** | Non-negotiable ethical floors |
| **Human Agency** | **Human Empowerment (Pillar 1)** | Direct mapping |

| **Ethics washing** | **Verification Theater** | Similar anti-pattern recognition |

### Novel Concepts from TUG

**1. Moral Foundations as "Paint"**
Dan: "Moral psychology is like the paint. Ethics is the painting you make with it."

Six moral foundations (from Haidt):
- Care / Harm prevention
- Fairness / Proportionality
- Loyalty / Betrayal
- Authority / Subversion
- Sanctity / Degradation
- Liberty / Oppression

**Methodology insight**: Understanding these foundations helps predict what ethical appeals will resonate. Also explains how ethics can be weaponized (pound all six foundations for persuasion).

**2. Verbs Over Nouns**
TUG framework uses verbs: "Cultivate," "Communicate," "Reflect" — not noun-based principles.

Dan: "From a polar bear book perspective... usually use nouns, but sometimes verbs are better."

**Application to Piper**: Our Four Pillars are noun-based (Empowerment, Integrity, Excellence, Boundaries). Could consider verb framing for operational guidance.

**3. Authentication vs. Authenticity**
Dan distinguishes:
- **Authentication**: Verifying identity (can be automated)
- **Authenticity**: Genuine experience communication (cannot be automated)

**Relevance**: Piper's professional boundaries are partly about not claiming authenticity it cannot have.

**4. Democratic Norms Connection**
Framework ultimately supports democratic processes — not just voting but discourse norms. Anti-propaganda, anti-bullshit (Frankfurt's technical definition: indifference to truth/ethics/authenticity).

## Methodology Enrichment Opportunities

### 1. Warranted Trust Vocabulary (High Value)

TUG's "warranted trust" framing is more precise than "trust gradient":
- Not "we want the technology to be trusted"
- But "we want people to have a warranted level of trust"
- Includes not having too much distrust

**Recommendation**: Adopt "warranted trust" as terminology in ADR-053 and ethics documentation. The concept is identical; the vocabulary is cleaner.

### 2. Three Validity Claims for Response Evaluation

Could add to Piper's response quality assessment:
- **Factual validity**: Is the response accurate?
- **Ethical validity**: Is it aligned with user/org ethos?
- **Authentic validity**: Are we appropriately NOT claiming authentic experience?

**Recommendation**: Consider for ResponsePersonalityEnhancer or quality metrics.

### 3. Ethics Scaffolds Governance Scaffolds Management

TUG's layering:
```
Ethics (why)
    ↓
Governance (policies/procedures)
    ↓
Management (operational practices)
```

**Application**: Piper's ethics documentation could be more explicit about this hierarchy. Our ADRs are governance; our patterns are management; our ethics docs are ethics.

### 4. Open Source Core + IP Extensions Model

Dan mentioned Creative Commons core vocabulary with potential IP extensions for consulting/services.

**Relevance**: Matches xian's stated vision for Piper Morgan — open source core, potential services layer.

## Key Insights from Discussion

### Ethical Pattern Portability (New Concept)

Ethical intuitions developed for human-to-human interactions often transfer to human-to-AI interactions — not by accident, but because AI's language capacity is built from human discourse. The transfer isn't automatic (authenticity doesn't port), but when it works, it works for good reasons.

**Implication**: What we learned about social interface design applies to AI interface design because AI agents are participating in social interfaces using human language.

### Complementary Ethics Scopes

| Scope | Framework | Focus |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Organizational | TUG | How humans interact ethically with AI |
| Agent-native | Piper | How AI behaves ethically toward humans |

These aren't separate — they connect across shared concepts (warranted trust, authenticity limits, ethics-as-infrastructure). Document the connection, not the separation.
