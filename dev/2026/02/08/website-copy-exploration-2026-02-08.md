## Working From

**Core insight**: PM tools assume work is items in lists. PM work is actually relationships between concerns at different scales.

**What resonated**: "Think bigger," "Piper holds the [X] so you can focus on the decision," "Your AI colleague for the work between the work."

**Problem to solve**: "Track everything" sounds like a todo app. "Context" is jargon.

## Hero Headline Options

### Direction A: Awareness / The Full Picture

| Option | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **See the whole picture** | Simple, clear. Risk: slightly generic. |
| **Nothing falls through** | Prevention-focused. Implies reliability. |
| **Stay ahead of it all** | Proactive. Risk: could sound like task management. |
| **See what others miss** | Differentiation baked in. Slightly competitive. |

### Direction B: Amplification / Think Bigger

| Option | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Think bigger** | Already resonated. Short, punchy. Opens the door to "how?" |
| **Think at the level that matters** | More specific. Implies Piper handles the lower levels. |
| **Focus where you're needed** | Outcome-oriented. You stay strategic. |
| **More you, more reach** | Explicit amplification. Might be too abstract. |

### Direction C: Cognitive Relief / Holding Capacity

| Option | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Hold it all — without holding it all** | Paradox that resolves with Piper. Conversational. |
| **Everything in mind, nothing on your mind** | Similar paradox, slightly more polished. |
| **Stop juggling. Start thinking.** | Clear benefit. Risk: "juggling" is a cliché. |
| **The mental bandwidth you've been missing** | Direct, but "bandwidth" might be jargon. |

### Direction D: Connections / Relationships

| Option | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **See the connections** | Simple, direct to the core insight. |
| **Everything's connected. Now you can see how.** | Two-part, narrative. |
| **The work is connections. Piper sees them.** | Very on-the-nose to the differentiator. |
| **Threads, not tasks** | Punchy contrast. Sets up the positioning. |

**My lean**: Direction B ("Think bigger") or Direction D ("See the connections") — both get at the core insight without sounding like task management.

## Positioning Paragraph Options

Expanding on "Your AI colleague for the work between the work."

### Option 1: Lead with the gap

> PM tools track tasks. But PM work isn't tasks — it's connections. Between teams, timelines, tradeoffs, and decisions at every scale. The work between the meetings. The thread between the projects. The pattern you'd see if you had time to step back.
>
> Piper is your AI colleague for all of it.

### Option 2: Lead with the pain

> You know the feeling: you're in a meeting and something important surfaces — but you can't remember the context. When did we decide that? What were the tradeoffs? Who else is affected?
>
> Piper holds those threads. Not because you can't — because you shouldn't have to. Your job is decisions. Piper's job is everything those decisions need.

### Option 3: Lead with the promise

> Piper is an AI colleague who actually understands PM work. Not just tasks and deadlines, but the relationships between them. The stakeholder who's affected by three different decisions. The timeline that's quietly drifting. The pattern you'd see if you could zoom out.
>
> Same you. More reach. Better decisions.

### Option 4: Lead with differentiation

> Task managers assume work is items in lists. But your work is connections — between people, projects, and priorities at every scale. Decisions ripple. Timelines interlock. Context matters.
>
> Piper is built for that reality. An AI colleague who holds the full picture so you can focus on the decision that matters right now.

**My lean**: Option 4 — it leads with the core insight ("connections, not lists") and lands on the value prop cleanly.

## Recommendations

1. **Headline**: "Think bigger" or "See the connections" — both avoid task-management framing while landing the core insight.

2. **Subhead**: "the full picture" is the cleanest "context" replacement. "The threads" is more evocative but might need support.

3. **Positioning**: Option 4 — leads with differentiation, lands on value prop.

4. **Overall tone**: Confident but not boastful. Colleague, not servant. Amplification, not replacement.
