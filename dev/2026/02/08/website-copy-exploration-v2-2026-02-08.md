## Where We've Landed

After initial exploration, PM and Comms aligned on:

- **Headline direction**: "Think bigger" — deceptively simple, works like "Think Different" (sounds like aspiration, actually means amplification)
- **Metaphor**: "threads" or "strands" over "connections" (more tactile, less corporate)
- **Positioning**: Lead with differentiation, not pain
- **Hero structure**: Two lines (headline + subhead), let it breathe

---

## Hero Headline

### Primary Candidate

> **Think bigger**

Why it works: Sounds like "dream big" but actually means "get more brainpower." The amplification message is baked in without being literal. Short. Confident. Opens the door.

### Alternate Candidates (if "Think bigger" doesn't land)

| Option | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **See the threads** | More specific, directly names what Piper does |
| **Hold more** | Amplification, but might be too abstract alone |
| **Same you, more reach** | Explicit amplification — maybe too on-the-nose |

---

## Hero Subhead

The subhead grounds the headline with the value prop.

### "Threads/strands" vocabulary options

| Option | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| the threads | Implies things you're already holding, Piper helps |
| the strands | Slightly softer, less "conspiracy board" energy |
| what you're juggling | Conversational but "juggling" is cliché |
| everything that connects | More abstract, explains threads |

### Full Subhead Candidates

**With "Think bigger":**

> Piper holds the threads so you can focus on the decision.

> Your AI colleague holds the strands. You hold the vision.

> Piper tracks what connects so you can think at the level that matters.

**My lean**: "Piper holds the threads so you can focus on the decision." — Clean, direct, delivers the value prop.

## Positioning Paragraph

Immediately below the hero. Expands on "the work between the work."

### Recommended Version (Option 4 from v1)

> Task managers assume work is items in lists. But your work is connections — between people, projects, and priorities at every scale. Decisions ripple. Timelines interlock. Context matters.
>
> Piper is built for that reality. An AI colleague who holds the full picture so you can focus on the decision that matters right now.

### Alternate: Shorter Version

> PM tools track tasks. PM work is connections.
>
> Piper is your AI colleague for that reality — the work between the work.
