### 1:42 PM - Hero Messaging Exploration

PM pondered hero message while CXO searched project knowledge for Ted Nadeau's Why/What frameworks.

**Ted's SQ Atoms identified the pain points:**
- Decision rationale scattered across tools
- Must remember exact identifiers (cognitive load)
- Standup summaries manually compiled
- Context decays over time
- Tool fragmentation

**PM's articulation of the meta-problem:**
> "A PM has to watch the big picture AND the P3 bugs, has to keep track of every dimension of a product AND focus on the core value proposition... There is no recipe book."

### Core Insight Emerged

> "PM tools assume work is items in lists. But PM work is actually relationships between concerns at different scales."

### What Resonated

- **Value prop direction**: "Think bigger. [comprehensive awareness]. Piper holds [your world] so you can focus on the decision." — Amplification framing works.
- **Positioning line**: "Your AI colleague for the work between the work." — Names the gap other tools miss.
- **Colleague framing**: Confirmed as correct (not partner, not assistant)

### What Needs Refinement (for Comms)

- "Track everything" — too generic, sounds like task management
- "Context" — LLM jargon, may confuse general audience
