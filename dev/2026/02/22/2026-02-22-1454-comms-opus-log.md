## 2:54 PM - Orientation & Week Review

### CXO Memo: Series Tagging Proposal

Received CXO memo proposing **visible structure, single voice** approach:

**Three series:**
- **The Method** — patterns, architecture, methodology deep-dives (technical-curious)
- **The Journey** — reflections, lessons, what-it-means pieces (leaders/identity)
- **The Product** — feature updates, how-to-try, what's new (potential users)

**Key insight**: The problem isn't that all audiences receive same content — it's that **readers can't efficiently self-select**. Series tags in subject lines let readers skim/skip without feeling like they're missing "the real content."

**CXO recommendation**: Implement series tagging on new posts now. Monitor engagement. Escalate to digest format only if data warrants.

**My take**: This is a smart middle path. Preserves the single authentic voice while giving readers navigation aids. Low editorial burden — tags are labels, not obligations. Worth piloting.
