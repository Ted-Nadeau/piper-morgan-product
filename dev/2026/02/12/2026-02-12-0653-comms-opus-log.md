## Pattern Observation: Confabulation Drift

This is the second post (after "The Calendar That Wasn't Mine") where narrative details were fabricated rather than sourced. The pattern:

1. **Inversion of causality** — Something real (file scoring tests #756-757) gets repositioned as the trigger when it was actually a side discovery
2. **Plausible but wrong specifics** — "73 columns" instead of 47, "three days" instead of one
3. **Narrative smoothing** — Details invented to make the story flow ("Pacific time is 8 hours behind UTC") that aren't in source material

**Root cause hypothesis:** When writing narratives, there's pressure to create a clean story arc. Without explicit source verification, the narrative fills gaps with plausible fabrication.

**Prevention:**
- Treat logs as authoritative text, not memory prompts
- When specifics aren't in logs, use placeholders
- PM review catches these, but earlier verification would save cycles
